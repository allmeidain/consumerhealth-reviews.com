Visivra Ingredients

Six ingredients carry this formula, and each one is aimed at a different piece of the same dimmer switch.

  • Zeaxanthin: Works alongside lutein to reinforce that same pigment layer, supporting sharper day-to-day visual clarity.
  • Bilberry Extract: Supports healthy ocular circulation and visual resilience, helping the eyes adapt to daily strain instead of just absorbing it.
  • Astaxanthin: A potent antioxidant that reinforces visual resilience and helps sustain long-term eye comfort.
  • Zinc: An essential mineral tied to healthy retinal function.
  • Vitamin A: The foundational nutrient behind normal visual function, present in this formula to keep that baseline covered.

Read together, it's not one flashy ingredient carrying the whole bottle: lutein and zeaxanthin reinforce the eye's own pigment shield, bilberry and astaxanthin work the circulation and resilience side, and zinc with vitamin A keep the foundational nutrients in place.
